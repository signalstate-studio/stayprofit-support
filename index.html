Frequently asked questions

Does StayProfit connect to Airbnb, Booking.com or other booking sites?

No. StayProfit is a planning tool. It uses the nightly rates, occupancy assumptions, booking channel shares, fees and costs you enter manually. It does not synchronize real bookings.

How are profit estimates calculated?

StayProfit estimates booked nights and revenue using your entered nightly rate and occupancy. It then deducts the applicable channel fee assumptions, co-host fees, operating and owner costs, and your selected estimated tax assumptions. Results are illustrative forecasts, not actual income records.

Are the tax calculations official?

No. Tax rates and calculation methods are user-selected planning assumptions. StayProfit is not a tax filing tool and does not determine your eligibility for a particular tax regime. Check local requirements with a qualified adviser.

Where is my data stored?

Your entered properties and saved scenarios are stored locally by the current version of the app. StayProfit does not provide an account or its own cloud synchronization service. Back up your information before deleting the app or changing devices.

How do I back up or restore my data?

Use the app’s Export backup (JSON) option to save a copy of your properties and scenarios. Use Restore backup to import a compatible file. Restoring a backup replaces the current properties and saved scenarios, so export your current data first.

How do I export a financial report?

Open the PDF financial report export option in StayProfit, then select where to save or share the generated file using the iOS share sheet.

Does the Calendar show my actual reservations?

No. Calendar and seasonal outlook features illustrate forecast scenarios. They are not connected to reservation calendars.

What should I do if the app crashes or my figures look wrong?

Check your input assumptions and channel percentages. If the issue persists, contact support with the steps to reproduce it. Export a backup before changing or reinstalling the app.
